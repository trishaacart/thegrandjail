Our Story

Behind The Bars At The Grand Jail

How a creative passion for hospitality created Rajgir's most unforgettable dining experience.

A Historic City, A Thrilling Culinary Experience

Rajgir is known worldwide for its spiritual serenity, ancient hot springs, and magnificent ruins of Magadha. But we wanted to give travelers, tourists, and locals something thrilling and completely unique: The Grand Jail Restaurant.

Step into a life-like prison ambiance complete with iron cell doors, prisoner uniforms, handcuffs, warden service, and vintage prison lamps. Every corner is crafted to spark laughter, playful photo shoots, and family memories you will talk about for years.
